## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:
ts farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose a digital marketplace that connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers directly, reducing the need for multiple intermediaries. Farmers can list their produce, quantity, quality, and expected price on the platform. Consumers and bulk buyers can compare prices and place orders directly.

The platform will include logistics support to coordinate pickup, storage, and delivery. AI-based demand forecasting will help predict the demand for agricultural products, while route optimization will help delivery partners choose efficient routes.

Innovation and uniqueness: The platform combines direct selling, transparent pricing, collective orders, logistics coordination, and AI-powered planning in one system.


## Technical Approach
Technologies to be used:

- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React

- Backend: Python with Django or Django REST Framework

- Database: PostgreSQL

- AI and data analysis: Python, Pandas, and Scikit-learn

- Maps and route planning: Mapping APIs and route optimization algorithms

- Notifications: SMS, email, or messaging integrations

Implementation methodology:

1. Collect requirements from farmers, FPOs, consumers, and bulk buyers.

2. Develop registration and login for different user roles.

3. Build product listing, search, price comparison, and order management features.

4. Integrate logistics scheduling and delivery tracking.

5. Use historical sales and seasonal data to forecast demand.

6. Implement route optimization based on delivery locations and vehicle capacity.

7. Test the prototype with sample agricultural products and simulated orders.

8. Conduct a pilot with selected farmers and buyers, then improve the platform using feedback.

## Feasibility and Viability
The solution is technically feasible using existing web development frameworks, databases, mapping services, and machine-learning libraries. A minimum viable product (MVP) can initially support registration, produce listings, orders, and basic logistics coordination. AI forecasting and advanced route optimization can be introduced in later stages.

Potential challenges:

- Limited digital literacy and internet access among some farmers.

- Seasonal variations in supply and demand.

- Perishable products requiring timely transportation and storage.

- Trust, payment security, and quality verification.

- Logistics costs, especially for small orders.

Strategies to overcome challenges:

- Provide a simple multilingual interface and assisted registration through FPOs.

- Use seasonal agricultural data and update forecasts as new sales information becomes available.

- Partner with local transport providers, warehouses, and cold-storage facilities where available.

- Introduce transparent grading, order records, secure payment options, and buyer reviews.

- Encourage group orders and shared transportation to reduce delivery costs.

The platform can generate revenue through a small transparent transaction fee, optional logistics service charges, or subscriptions for bulk buyers, while keeping farmer charges affordable.


## Impact and Benefits
Economic impact:

- Helps farmers access more buyers and negotiate better prices.

- Reduces avoidable intermediary costs.

- Gives consumers and retailers access to competitive prices.

- Improves market access for small farmers and FPOs.

Social impact:

- Supports rural livelihoods and digital inclusion.

- Strengthens connections between farming communities and urban markets.

- Improves price transparency and access to market information.

Environmental impact:

- Optimized delivery routes can reduce unnecessary travel and fuel use.

- Better demand forecasting can help reduce overproduction and food waste.

- Coordinated collection and distribution can improve resource utilization.

Expected outcome: A scalable and transparent agricultural marketplace that aims to improve farmer earnings, offer fairer prices to consumers, and make the agricultural supply chain more efficient.
